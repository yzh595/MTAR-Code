## 🔎 Introduction

**MTAR++** improves autoregressive image generation through spatial foresight supervision and pretrained generative knowledge transfer. Built on MTAR, it enriches training supervision while keeping the original autoregressive inference procedure unchanged.

- **Foresight supervision:** predict spatially related future tokens and features.
- **Generative knowledge transfer:** reuse representations from an existing pretrained and frozen teacher.
- **Efficient training:** reduce redundant computation through dual-path semantic dropping, without additional inference overhead.

### Training efficiency

MTAR achieves up to 1.39× training-time speedup at the same 300-epoch budget. After 50 epochs, MTAR++(B) and MTAR++(L) achieve FIDs of 5.35 and 3.53, respectively, surpassing their corresponding 300-epoch LlamaGen baselines.

### Data efficiency

With all models trained for 50 epochs, MTAR++(B) using 300k student images reaches FID 6.53, compared with 7.29 for LlamaGen-B using 1.281M images. The generative teacher is pretrained on the full ImageNet training set.

## 🛠️ Method

MTP predicts future codebook indices; MFP transfers a frozen generative teacher's current- and future-position features. Both retain next-token supervision and incorporate token-level contrastive regularization.

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/mtarpp_foresight_overview.png" width="960" alt="Complete multi-token foresight supervision diagram: (a) MTAR multi-token prediction of future codebook indices; (b) MTAR++ multi-feature prediction of current and spatially related future features from a frozen generative teacher.">
  <br><sub>(a) Multi-token prediction in MTAR. (b) Multi-feature prediction in MTAR++.</sub>
</p>

Semantic dropping reduces redundant token computation. Its dual-path extension compresses student and teacher sequences while preserving complete teacher targets at shared supervision anchors.
